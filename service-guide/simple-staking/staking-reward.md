# Staking reward

To receive unclaimed staking rewards, move them to "Your Staked" by following these steps:

* "Unclaimed Staking Reward" area displays the staking rewards you haven't received yet&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2-L2operator (1).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The corresponding amount is now added to your staking amount, i.e., Your Staked amount&#x20;
* The Unclaimed Staking Reward area remains with a balance of 0.
