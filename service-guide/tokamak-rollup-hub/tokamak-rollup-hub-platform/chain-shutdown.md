# Chain Shutdown

Overview:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-03 at 5.31.32 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Typical Duration (10-15 min) :**\
**Note: The shutdown process must be handled carefully if you are operating on mainnet.**

1. A deployed and active chain can be deleted at any time by clicking the bin icon for that chain.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-03 at 5.32.31 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. The chain destruction process will begin, and you can monitor the logs in the Deployment History tab.
