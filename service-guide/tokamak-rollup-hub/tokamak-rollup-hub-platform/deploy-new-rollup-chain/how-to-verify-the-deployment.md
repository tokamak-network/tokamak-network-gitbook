# How to verify the Deployment?

1.  After clicking the _Deploy Rollup_ button, you can monitor the chain deployment on the Rollup tab page.<br>

    ![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-05 at 3.42.11 PM.png>)
2.  To view real-time deployment logs, click on the chain and check its deployment history.<br>

    ![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-05 at 3.42.56 PM.png>)
3.  You can watch the real time logs by clicking on the logs button.<br>

    ![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-03 at 2.44.33 PM.png>)
4. Once the chain is successfully deployed, the deployment history will be shown like below.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-04 at 10.28.22 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. You can also view integrations through the _Integrations_ tab. By default, the bridge is installed with every chain deployment. If you provided details to register a candidate during deployment, the Staking/DAO candidate integration will also be displayed, as shown in the screenshot below.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-05 at 3.43.58 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
