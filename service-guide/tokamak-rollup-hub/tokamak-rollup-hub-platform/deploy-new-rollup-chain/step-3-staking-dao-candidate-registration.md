# Step 3 - Staking/ DAO Candidate Registration

Note: This Step is optional and could be skipped

In this step, the operator provides the necessary information to register their chain with Tokamak Staking v2, enabling them to receive [seigniorage](https://github.com/tokamak-network/papers/blob/master/cryptoeconomics/tokamak-cryptoeconomics-en.md#2-seigniorage) in the future.

1.  Enter the amount of TON you wish to stake. Ensure that the admin account selected in the [previous step](https://docs.tokamak.network/home/service-guide/tokamak-rollup-hub/tokamak-rollup-hub-platform/deploy-new-rollup-chain/step-2-account-and-aws-configuration) holds this balance. The minimum staking requirement is 1000.1 TON. You may request TON faucets in Sepolia from - [Deployed Contract](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/address/0xd655762c601b9cac8f6644c4841e47e4734d0444#writeContract#F1) ([Guide](https://docs.tokamak.network/home/service-guide/faucet-testnet))<br>

    ![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-05 at 2.56.04 PM.png>)
2. Please provide a memo to appear in the staking v2 dashboard (mandatory)
3. Please provide a name (optional)
4. Click Next in the platform
