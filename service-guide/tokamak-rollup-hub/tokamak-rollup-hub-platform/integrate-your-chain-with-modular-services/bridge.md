# Bridge

1. Bridge is installed by default after a chain deployment. You can find the bridge link in quick links section in the respective chain overview.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-08 at 4.11.38 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. If you wish to uninstall the bridge, you could do it in the integration tab section.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-08 at 4.31.32 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Clicking on the bin icon will uninstall the bridge and you can install it again later.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-08 at 4.12.13 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
